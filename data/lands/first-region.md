# Land presets

Lands set by hand from a few numbers (A5.6, `WLD-34`): until whole worlds are generated (`MIL-04`), play and test scenes run on them.
Each is an island in a sea nobody can cross, its fixed layers built by the same rules generation will use, so nothing is thrown away when the generator arrives (`PRN-09`).

## The first region

An island about 60 km across at about 46° north, in a sea 50 km wide on every side: uplands to the north, a river valley running from north to south, and across the island's north a chalk escarpment with caves, over sandstone and granite.
Broadleaf woods on loam, a mild climate with cold winters, and herds of red deer and wild boar, with wolves.
The river's flow is fixed until routing runs (A5.10); the herds and small game wait for their animals (α12b, α12c).
