## World

How long a new world settles before history begins (A4.1, `WLD-08`); the factors that scale yields and animal numbers to the small world (A5.13, `WLD-30`), read from α04a; and where streams and rivers begin and how wide and deep a river runs for its flow (A5.7 step 5, A5.10).

An escarpment has a gap people can climb every 3 km.
