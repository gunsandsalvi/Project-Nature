# Looks

Each material's look (A11.3, `PRE-20`): its colour under white light, from which the light model makes its ladder of steps at any hour, and how many steps the ladder has.
The colours are reflectances, put in sRGB: measured where the sources give them, and plausible estimates otherwise (`PRN-05`).

## Lush grass

Green grass in leaf.
Leaves reflect little blue and red, which chlorophyll takes, and a little more green: about 4, 12 and 5 percent of blue, green and red light.
Six steps, since grass covers most of the land and its shading carries the shape of the ground.
