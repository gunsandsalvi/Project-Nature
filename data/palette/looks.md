# Looks

Each material's look (A11.3, `PRE-20`): its colour under white light, from which the light model makes its ladder of steps at any hour, and how many steps the ladder has.
The colours are reflectances, put in sRGB: plausible values from field measurements (`PRN-05`), as tuned in the example picture of this light the owner approved on 3 October 2026.

## Lush grass

Green grass in leaf.
Leaves reflect little blue and red, which chlorophyll takes, and more green: about 4, 15 and 8 percent of blue, green and red light.
Six steps, since grass covers most of the land and its shading carries the shape of the ground.
