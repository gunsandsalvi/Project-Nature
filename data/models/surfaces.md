# Surfaces

The ground's surfaces (A11.5, `PRE-20`): each names the looks it is drawn in, one to three, and how a world-fixed noise splits them between its looks, so patches stay where they are and keep their edges at every zoom.
A surface marked `rock` draws in the rock category, whose outlines are finer than the ground's (A11.2).

## Grass

Grass over soil, wherever the ground is gentle: green in leaf, with patches gone to straw where it is drier.
A world-fixed noise of 24 m and 6 m octaves splits the two looks; dry grass takes the noise above 0.25, about a fifth of the ground in spring.
