### 1. [blocker] A6.8, A6.9 Choosing reads the catalogue's hidden values, and a mistaken fit has no outcome
Problem: A6.8 prunes `find_inputs` with `by_kind` and each role's "kinds that can fit", both built at catalogue load from true values, and answers map places with `cell_offers` from true deposits and today's ripe yields. A8.13 and A8.15 require choosing on believed values and the person's own map facts only (`MND-02`, `MND-04`), and `PRN-01` and `PRN-07` ("see only what people perceive and know") forbid anything else. As written, nobody ever tries a look-alike that fails, and everyone knows which far cell has ripe berries today. When a believed fit is false, A6.9's `plan` returns `NoFit` and nothing says what follows: if Choose runs again, the same records give the same pick (a loop), and the true value is never learned.
Fix: A6.8: `find_inputs(bp, &InReach)` checks hidden characteristics only against `InReach`'s believed values; the true-value index may prune only by class, form, size, states and characteristics known on sight. Remove `cell_offers` from choosing: cell contents reach minds only as map facts made when someone notices a cell (A5.2 `CellNotice`) or visits it. A6.9: a believed fit that isn't true still starts; each try fails as `nothing`, and settle reports `Seen::Wrong { kind, chars }`, which A8.13 records as learned values (`MND-04`, "by use"). A8.22 adds a scene: a look-alike stone is struck, fails, and is then left alone.

### 2. [major] A6.9 One event per activity contradicts `TIM-17` and A4.4
Problem: A6.9's plan knows every try's outcome from the start, reads the doer's state once, and lands all results at the end or an interruption ("one event per activity"). `TIM-17` (Decided) says each flake lands as its strike ends, and A4.4 gives each repeat its own End event with `go_on`. As written, the first flake can't be picked up, watched (A8.19 counts uses) or drawn as it comes off (A4.13) until the activity ends; pain, cold or tiredness arising mid-activity are ignored; and a blessing or curse given mid-activity (A10.4) either can't touch the later tries or makes settle disagree with the plan. A4 and A6 would be built against each other.
Fix: A6.9 follows A4.4: `plan` at the start checks fit and marks inputs busy; each try is one End event that rolls `things.try` (keyed on the doer and that try's end second, A3.3) with the doer's state then, settles that try (result, failures, leftovers, wear, used-up mass) and calls `go_on` (inputs left, tool unbroken, no hurt, repeats allowed). Side fits roll once, at the last try. A6.16's cost becomes ≤ 1 µs a try plus ≤ 4 µs for side fits, with 0.1 µs of heap work a try (A4.3). Also: A4.4's `Wip` and A6.7's `WorkRow` are one record (A6's name), settled at the most skilled worker's level (`MAT-04`), not "the finisher's"; a stale fire event is dropped by its stamp (A4.3), not its sequence number.
Part: P4 (A4.4 takes the name)

### 3. [major] A8.21, A8.9, A8.2 Per-window caches break save-and-reload determinism
Problem: A8.21 bounds a village's cost by "each cluster's per-window cache of what is in reach"; A8.9 caches line of sight "per cluster and window"; A8.2 keeps a "worth cache" rebuilt at most daily. A3.1 rule 8 allows only caches whose hit equals a miss, never saved. Things are taken, made and moved, pits dug and huts built inside a window, so a hit late in the window differs from a fresh look; a save at a paused second (A4.8 saves no caches) and a reload refill them from the new state, and `kd det`'s mid-window save test fails. The 6 GiB pause (A4.11) "drops every cache that can go", which would drop the worth table and change choices.
Fix: In reach: no per-window snapshot; `in_reach` reads A6.8's per-area kind-and-bucket index, kept current by every `put` and `remove`, with a `u32` version per bucket, and any cached answer is keyed on the versions it read. Sight: key the cache on (from bucket, to bucket, area record version). Worth: rename it the worth table, saved in `people.lists`, rebuilt only at the wake job, never dropped.
Part: P5

### 4. [major] A6.14, A6.9, A8.17 The Discoverable check doesn't match how discovery happens in play
Problem: The check can pass while an arc step can't be found. (1) Its accident route adds "one resting thing", but nothing in A6.9 or A8.15 says what an activity rests on unless a role asks for it, so in play an activity holds `find_inputs`' set or an experiment's one or two things. (2) Its hint route counts for blueprints of three or more roles (hafted spear, sewn cloak, hut), but a hunch holds at most two input kinds (A8.17), and nothing says what else a hunch try picks up. (3) It omits the `means` timer route that A6.10 and `MOM-12` use for copper. (4) For `work` and timer blueprints, a side fit's success "comes too where the things allow" (A6.9), so ten minutes of stacking could finish a 12 person-hour hut, or nothing could.
Fix: (1) A6.8: an activity's things are its inputs plus what the worked thing rests on (the largest thing within 0.5 m under it, else the ground); the check models exactly this. (2) A8.17: a hunch keeps up to 4 input classes, from the hinting event's things or `made_marks`; a hunch try brings one thing in reach per class, plus the held tool and the rest; A6.14 counts a hint only when those fill every role. (3) A6.14 adds the route "a timer the blueprint `means` runs on reachable things in a reachable setting". (4) A6.9: an unknown `work` or timer blueprint's success, if noticed, teaches it at skill 1 and opens its `WorkRow` or timer with this activity's hours; it never makes the result at once.
Part: P4, P5

### 5. [major] A8.23 The milestone split needs four pieces earlier, or stand-ins get thrown away

To `MIL-04`: a plan's request reaches members as a call (A4.7, built at `MIL-02`) within hearing; talk adds second-hand hearing at `MIL-05`.

### 7. [major] A6.4 Old camps never shrink: `MAT-08`'s merging on burial is missing
Problem: `MAT-08` (Decided) says loose leftovers merge into heaps as they are buried; A6.4 merges only like things (kind, material, states, quality) when put within 0.5 m. A6.4 expects about 500 live things a person (1 million at 2,000 people); a band leaving a seasonal camp leaves most of them behind, and stone, fired clay and copper never go. After centuries of camps the dormant kept areas hold tens of millions of things, far past A5.4's 2 KB average a dormant area and `PLT-04`'s 1 GiB after 500 years.
Fix: A6.4 adds burial merging: at each kept area's catch-up, and yearly while active, loose things on the ground over a game year old and buried over 2 cm merge, per 4 m patch, by (class, form, material) into one heap whose amount and mass are their sum, keeping the lowest uid; made things of quality 3 or more, art, grave goods and named things keep their identity. Test: in the Year-500 pace worlds, dormant kept areas average under 2 KB.
Part: P4

### 8. [major] A7.3 Planted plants multiply into marks that never fade
Problem: A7.3 gives each planted plant (a mark) "each season one spreading draw [that] places a new one", itself a planted mark, and A5.4's mark table has no row for planted plants, so none fades. One planted tree or one sprouting heap (`MOM-08`) multiplies season by season up to 5,000 marks an area and spreads into neighbouring areas, which then stay kept forever, never "unchanged again" (`WLD-12`), each stepped daily: against A5.4's 2 KB a dormant area, `PLT-04`'s 1 GiB, and A16.3's 25 µs an active area.
Fix: A7.3: spread from planted plants never makes marks; it raises the cell's `people_cover` share of that species (A5.4), which the cover step grows by its own rules. Only what people put in the ground, or what sprouts from their seed, is a mark, at most 256 an area; the mark fades when the plant dies, or 5 game years after it is grown and untended, its spot then following the seed and the cell's cover. A5.4 gains that row.
Part: P4 (the row in A5.4 is P3's)

### 9. [major] A6.12, A7.10 A timer group's step touches every row, no budget counts it, and traps have no clock
Problem: A6.12 keeps one event per group, but "a step brings progress up to now and resorts, about 0.3 µs a row", so every rain start or stop and every temperature-band change rewrites every timer row in the area; the stated reason ("weather touches one group per area, not every thing") doesn't hold. With about 1 million live things at 2,000 people, many organic, and 4–6 condition steps a day, that is roughly 0.1–0.5 core-seconds a game day, in no line of A16.3 (only "≤ 1 µs per end"). Set traps and baited lines (A7.10) roll "each night" or "each hour" with no event or catch-up named, so a trap left over a day in a dormant area never catches.
Fix: Per group and timer kind, keep one shared progress clock G(t) = ∫ condition factor dt, advanced in O(1) at each step; a row stores its own constant factor (from its states) and the G value at which it is due, sorted once; a condition step changes G's rate and the group's one event only; a row is re-inserted only when its own thing changes. Rain wets things as a group condition, not by flipping `wet` on every thing at each shower. A set trap or line is a timer row whose rolls are keyed on (trap uid, night or hour) and rolled in order at catch-up. A16.3 adds timer groups: ≤ 0.5 µs a step, ≤ 1 µs a row insert.
Part: P4

### 10. [major] A7.7, A7.8, A7.9 Herds with animals out, followers' cost and the daily slot
Problem: (1) A7.7's daily step ignores animals out as individuals (A7.8): it still moves the herd's leg and eats and dies by full counts while the individuals graze and move by their own minds, so food is eaten twice and the herd folds back at a cell its animals never reached. (2) A7.9 prices a follower at about 1 µs a day, but every animal near people holds activities (`TIM-17`; A4.4: ≤ 2 µs a start or end), and tame and kept animals choose alone (`MND-16`), so a `MIL-07` kept herd costs about 50 µs an animal. (3) Herds step "daily at dawn" (A7.7), at 12:00 (A4.9) or at each region's own barrier (A5.9); a herd moving into a region whose slot has passed or is still due steps twice or not at all that day.
Fix: (1) While `out > 0`, the daily step skips the out animals' eating, deaths and births and doesn't move the herd; the herd's cell and leg follow its lead individual, and it folds back where the lead is. (2) Followers hold no activity events of their own: their activity and position are the lead's at their offset, worked out when read, with events only for their own Body thresholds and for leaving the herd; kept and tame animals are priced at 50 µs in A16.3, and the `MIL-07` village benchmark includes a kept herd of 50. (3) A herd steps once a game day at its home region's slot (A5.9), keeping `last_day` so it never steps twice; A7.7 drops "at dawn".

### 11. [major] A6.11, A7.5 A people's fire never reaches the grass, so it never reaches its area's edge
Problem: `MAT-18` has campfires creep along the ground unless ringed, and A6.11 and A5.9 start a cell fire when a people's fire reaches its area's edge. But A6.11 spreads only to things (within 1 m, sparks 3 m), and A7.5's in-area front starts only from an entry edge; ground cover and litter are 4 m patches, not things. So no campfire ever escapes, nobody can burn land on purpose (`WLD-28`: "theirs to discover"), and the hearth ring stops nothing.
Fix: A6.11: a fire of level 2 or more whose `setting` is `open` lights the 4 m patch it stands on, if the patch has cover, after that cover's catch time (as for things, by burn) × A7.5's dryness. A7.5: a front may start at an inner patch, each patch then burning at its shortest-path time from it; reaching the area's edge hands over to A5.9's cell fire, as now. Scene: an open campfire in dry grass and wind reaches the area's edge in some runs, a ringed one never.

### 13. [minor] A6.1, A7.1 The trait surfaces don't cover the calls A6, A7 and A8 make to each other

### 16. [minor] A7.13, A8.6 The single death roll misses resistance and differs between parts
Problem: `BIO-05` also scales the death chance by resistance ("each fifth of resistance below or above average adds or takes off a fifth of it"); neither A7.13's list nor A8.6's `risk_factors` has it. The carer factor differs three ways: A7.13 "0.25 with a master healer", A8.6 "×0.25 with healing experience 8 or more", `BIO-05` "down to a quarter as the carer's healing experience nears 10".
Fix: A7.13 states the roll once: `death` × 5 (under 1 or over 60) × 2 for each of hungry (condition under 30), shivering and wounded (pain 20 or more) × (0.5 − 0.025 × the carer's healing experience, if cared for that day) × (1 − 0.1 × medicine taken that day) × (1 + (average − resistance) ÷ average), at most 0.95. A8.6's `risk_factors` returns these inputs, resistance included, and cites A7.13 instead of giving its own numbers.

### 17. [minor] A8.11, A8.15, A8.18 Four mind rules lack the numbers a builder needs
Problem: (1) Feelings have triggers and half-lives but no set-off sizes, though fear of 50 or more marks feared places (A8.12, `MND-08`), raises risk cost and cuts glances. (2) Reasons are "the three score parts that most put the winner ahead of the next best", undefined when the pick draws a close second or the activity is forced (a breakdown, a flight). (3) "Opinion +1 a window × likeness" has no likeness rule. (4) The talk topic is picked "by what the teller holds and the listeners lack, their feelings and relations, and their plans", with no weights.
Fix: (1) `data/minds/feelings.md` gives set-off sizes: fear 40 + 50 × (1 − distance ÷ range) for danger seen, plus a wound's size, 80 for an attack, × 1.5 for a ready fear; anger the harm's size, 30 for an insult; joy 30 a success, 60 a birth, 40 a feast; shame 30; awe 60; a new set-off keeps the larger of old and new. (2) A close second's first reason is "chance among close options", its parts compared with the best; a forced activity keeps its cause as its one reason. (3) Likeness = 1 − mean |trait difference| ÷ 6. (4) Topic score = its weight in `data/minds/topics.md` × the listeners lacking it (who knows what), + 10 for a plan request, highest first.
