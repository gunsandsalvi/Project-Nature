### 3. [major] PRN-11 PRN-12 WLD-12 "Its pace grows coarser" reads as cutting detail during play
Problem: `WLD-12` says "If a layer runs over its share (`PLT-04`), its pace grows coarser everywhere alike".
Read as a rule of the running game, the world then runs differently with the phone's load.
That breaks `PRN-11` (when the phone can't keep up, time slows and detail stays, with the same results at every speed), `PRN-12` (rules never change during play) and `RES-05` (exact repeats on phone and cloud, whose loads differ).
Read as a fix made after a stage's measurements, it is fine.
Two builders would build different things, and the wrong one breaks exact repeats (`RSK-04`).
Fix: `WLD-12`, replace the sentence with: "If a layer runs over its share in a stage's measurements (`PLT-04`), its pace is set coarser from the next build, the same for every world, speed and phone, never during play and never as less detail where people are (`PRN-11`, `PRN-12`)."

### 4. [major] MOM-12 MAT-19 TIM-19 Nothing gives potters a reason to blow their kilns, so copper has no route in whole worlds
Problem: The only route to smelted copper is a charcoal kiln blown through pipes for an hour, with a blower at each pipe in turns (`MAT-18`, `RCK-08`).
`MOM-12` says potters do this "to hurry a slow firing".
But `MAT-19` gives firing only "at heat 3 for about 4 hours ..., tougher at 4".
Its general line, that a timer is "sped up, slowed or stopped by conditions", with heat among them, gives firing no number.
A charcoal kiln already reaches heat 4 without blowing.
People choose by what an option does for their needs (`MND-09`), so hours of shared blowing that gain nothing are never chosen.
The scene can set up the habit (`RES-16`), but whole worlds must reach copper in Years 300–500 (`TIM-19`, `RES-07`), and `MIL-07` closes only when every pace target is met.
Fix: G4, `MAT-19` Firing adds: "a firing hotter than it needs runs about twice as fast for each level above (tuned), so a kiln blown to heat 5 fires pots in about an hour".
`MOM-12` step 1 becomes: "Potters who have seen that a hotter kiln fires sooner (`MAT-19`, `MND-04`) blow into their charcoal kilns through reed or clay pipes, so they reach heat 5 (`MAT-18`, `RCK-22`)."
Cross-section: G4 `MAT-19`.

### 5. [major] PRN-07 MAT-17 "Routes nobody listed" contradicts the Expected fits check
Problem: `PRN-07` (and brief 3.6) promise that generic blueprints let "people find routes nobody listed".
Round 1 added `MAT-17`'s Expected fits check: each item and blueprint names what it is meant to fit, and any other fit fails until it is named or the ranges are narrowed.
So every route is listed before it can exist.
A builder following `PRN-07` lets unlisted fits through, and one following `MAT-17` blocks them.
Principles win (`PRN-16`), so as written the check must go, though it is the only automated guard against absurd fits (`RSK-06`).
Fix (keeps the check): `PRN-07`, replace "So one blueprint works for everything that fits, and people can find routes nobody listed." with "So one blueprint works for everything that fits, and which route a people finds depends only on what they have and try; the catalogue check names every fit in advance, so none is absurd (`MAT-17`)."
If the brief's "routes the designers didn't list" must stand word for word, change `MAT-17` instead: the check flags fits nobody named for review, and fails only those a reality rule rejects.
Cross-section: G4 `MAT-17` (only in the alternative).
