# M3 owner decisions

Q1–Q10 answered on 8 October 2026, as recorded in OWNER-ANSWERS.md. Q11/Q12 remain for the later M3 review. These answers settle the listed decisions; they do not claim implementation, phone results or repository adoption.

### Q1. When do checks needing later milestones count?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** phase acceptance as recommended. M3 proves the physical world; M4 completes living settling, resource renewal and buried traces; M5 completes bands and winter survival. Every original check stays mapped and open until proved. A water-only run or synthetic archaeological fixture does not pass the full later check.

### Q2. May areas be exactly 250 m?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** yes. Four by four 250 m areas tile each exact 1 km cell, with 251-vertex metre grids including shared borders. The exact M1 torus remains unchanged.

### Q3. How is Earth's climate compared?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** freeze the method before tuning. Use 10-degree latitude bands, at most 10 percentage points of climate-class share difference per sufficiently sampled band, and about 2°C matched-bin temperatures; match altitude, maritime/current exposure and declare weighting/scaled distances. Use an Earth-tilt case plus 15°/30° cases.

The proposed dry-day and dry-spell definitions are accepted: below 1 mm rain/day is dry; the saved longest-dry-spell envelope is the 95th percentile of annual maxima. Storm-day/rate shares follow RES-13. The separate every-place, 20-year bounds remain 10% rain and 1°C against the world's own climate. Reference data and event thresholds still must be frozen before tuning.

### Q4. Do worlds have mammoths?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** yes, one woolly mammoth species in cold open grassland, within the roughly 30 wild-species budget. M4 proves its food demand and density. The detailed catalogue entry and art still follow their normal reviews; no ice-age or extinction system is added.

### Q5. What qualifies a start?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** at least 20% estimated food margin in each season without double-counting overlapping foraging ranges; several food kinds; 3–4 dry shelters for 15–30 people each; 2 m² usable dry floor per person as a tunable estimate; perennial water within 2 km and flaking stone within 10 km. M4 yields and M5 bodies must confirm the starting-kit estimates and actual survival.

### Q6. How many eclipses count?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** **3–8 visible solar or lunar eclipses at every place in 70 game years**, clouds or not. This replaces the recommended 2–5. Total solar eclipses are not promised everywhere.

Count appreciable partial/total alignments above the geometric horizon; report obscuration separately. Sampling is diagnostic only: final acceptance covers every place, including visibility boundaries. The 15-day phase cycle stays fixed. Inclination, node precession, apparent sizes and the visibility threshold remain orbit parameters to fit and validate; the draft has no fitted parameter set proving this range yet. Do not change the requirement if fitting fails; report the failure.

### Q7. What timing and map definitions apply?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** 180 seconds to choices and 60 more to the selected world, with outer limits 198/66 seconds. Include fallback search, qualification, previews and entry saving at held phone speed; never stop search by wall-clock time.

The proposed measurement definitions are accepted: 1 km land-cell slope for the global gate, fine-slope/cliff diagnostics separately, lake area excluding sea/permanent ice, and coastline straightness on 1 km contours with a 500 m chord-deviation tolerance. Other “about” timing limits use the stated 10% interpretation.

### Q8. What memory line applies?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** a 2 GiB M3 peak-process working line, retaining M2's separate texture/target/staging limits. The initial arithmetic ledger is about 1.46 GiB; real allocations and save overlap still need measurement. Final-game, kept-area and old-world storage targets remain unchanged.

### Q9. How does weather use the short year?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** weather uses mm per real-length game day; biome classification uses Earth-equivalent annual precipitation/growing-degree indices; seasonal totals cover 15 game days. Apply compressed dry-spell recession to reserves, not wind or river travel. Rare-event rates are per game year.

### Q10. How are flint and chert named?

**Answered — 8 October 2026:** flint only from chalk, chert from eligible limestone, with source provenance retained in river gravels. Both count as the flaking-stone family; each keeps its precise name.

### Q11. Does the generated world look believable and varied?

**Pending — later M3 review:** the owner judges held-out worlds at every zoom, including rivers/lakes, mountain-to-plain transitions, coasts, rock sections and seams. Numerical gates and visual acceptance are both required. Remaining detailed pass rules must be fixed before tuning; loosening an approved rule requires agreement.
