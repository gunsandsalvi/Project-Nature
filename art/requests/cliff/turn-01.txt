HOW THE ENGINE SHOWS A WALL
A texture pixel (a texel) is shown about 2 x 2 screen pixels at the closest zoom (band 0) and each band after halves the texels a metre. The camera looks 37 degrees down, so a vertical wall's texels are shown about 0.8 as tall as wide. A rock face is a vertical surface covered with its rock's tile, each 4 m cell of the wall taking one of four versions of the tile by chance; the ground lies at its foot.
