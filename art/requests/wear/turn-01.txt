HOW THE ENGINE SHOWS THE GROUND
A texture pixel (a texel) is shown about 2 x 2 screen pixels at the closest zoom (band 0), and each band after halves the texels a metre (band 2 has a quarter, band 4 a sixteenth). The camera looks 37 degrees down, so every texel is shown only about 0.6 as tall as wide. The material is meant to be quiet: a calm mottled surface of crumbs, with a few small stones, and none of the marks of the cracked earth or the gravel.
