# The look's targets

Pictures that show the feeling the graphics engine aims for (`MIL-09`, research 19).
They are targets and guides, never shipped and never traced: the engine is judged against their light, colour, density and composition, not copied from them.
Every one was drawn by GPT's image tool on 6 October 2026, from the prompt beside it in `prompts/`, and is kept here as WebP.

Each is checked for Stone Age truth before anyone aims at it (research 19, study 1 section 6.4).
Things marked "not to copy" are later or borrowed things GPT drew.
