# The art book: preliminary pictures

**These pictures were preliminary.** They were painted quickly in pre-production (October 2026) to find a first direction for the look: 3D drawn as crisp pixel art, light in clean steps, coloured outlines and lit edges, people and animals of blocks, and one place seen from a person up to the globe.
They are where the look started, not where it ends. The graphics engine (`MIL-09`) must go well beyond them in every way that shows: composition, light, colour, materials, detail, motion, and how alive the world feels.
They are not the bar the game is judged against: the bar is set by the engine's own scenes, which you judge (`PRE-31`).

The code that painted them was deleted on 6 October 2026, as you asked, and git keeps it; nothing is ported from it (`CLAUDE.md`).

What is here, each picture at its true size, one art pixel to one pixel (enlarge them by whole numbers with hard edges):
- `plates/scenes`: a scene for each age of the arc.
- `plates/sheets`: sheets of the model kit: people, animals, plants, things, light and scale.
- `plates/ui`: the interface: the play screen, a card, the book of ages, the landscape layout, the powers and the worlds.
- `plates/zoom`: one place at each zoom stop of `PRE-03`, from one person to the globe, at noon and dusk, and held sideways: a band's camp under a cliff by a river.
- `plates/options`: the same crops in the looks and water styles you chose between in pre-production.
